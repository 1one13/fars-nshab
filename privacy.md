# Privacy Policy - Fars Uploader

**Fars Uploader** is a private tool used only by the owner of the Fars Nshab channels to upload horseback-archery videos to the Fars Nshab YouTube channel.

- It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and read the channel's own video list.
- - It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. It has no other users.
